[It Taks Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/It_Takes_Two_(video_game)) is one of the best two-player cooperation games now. I played this game with my girlfriend in 23-24. 

This game fit the LEGO setting because of it's plot happening in the mini-world of the doll size. In that I wondered if I can turn the topic into a LEGO set after this semester's finals. 

I designed the frog taxis and their station in the game's chapter 6. For the main part, I used the PartDesigner to formulate minifigures for Cody and May.

The adorable queen elephant impressed me so I made it as an icon as well.

To see a full introduction of the design, click [bilibili](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1gjK96XEsu/?spm_id_from=333.1387.homepage.video_card.click) to watch!
