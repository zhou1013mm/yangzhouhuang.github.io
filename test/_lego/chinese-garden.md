This is my second LEGO MOC. I have published it on [LEGO IDEAS](https://beta.ideas.lego.com/) The [Chinese Garden](https://ideas.lego.com/product-ideas/c09e02be-c442-47e2-bd42-7eca541eb08b) was inspired by the Suzhou Yuanlin in Suzhou, China. If you're interested in the physical prototype of the job, please refer to Zhuozheng Yuan ([拙政园](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%8B%99%E6%94%BF%E5%9B%AD)) and Liu Yuan ([留园](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/%E7%95%99%E5%9B%AD)).

I went to Suzhou in 2023 when the beauty and tranquil atmosphere of Su-style Yuanlin (苏式园林) impressed me. As I finished my first LEGO IDEAS job [Chrono Trigger Robo](https://ideas.lego.com/product-ideas/204a285e-836b-4aaf-8a81-8c6b75f0d489) before, I wondered if I could also put this way of desing into something about China, because I found not many great works shared on LEGO IDEAS are about my country. 

So, here it is. I designed it in size of minifigures, which makes it more interactable and fun to play. The set totally contains more than 5500 brick parts, 8 minifigures. I used the [PartDesigner](https://www.bricklink.com/v3/studio/partdesigner.page) to create several printed 2x4 tile as Chinese Ink Wash Paintings ([水墨画](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%B0%B4%E5%A2%A8%E7%94%BB)). 

The water pavillion in the set is nearly copied from Zhuozheng Yuan, in which it's called Ta Ying Ting ([塔影亭](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E5%A1%94%E5%BD%B1%E4%BA%AD/59076909)). The aesthetic center of the set is the pavillion. The tallest tree is beautiful also. 

After the job, I still have something to try in this topic. Rockery ([假山](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%81%87%E5%B1%B1)) is a core element in Chinese Garden designing, but I think I didn't make it outstanding in this set. Maybe I can do better in the future.
