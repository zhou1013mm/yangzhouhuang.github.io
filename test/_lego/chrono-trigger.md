Here I present my first LEGO MOC, the Robo from the highest ranked [JRPG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Eastern_role-playing_video_games#Japanese_role-playing_games), [Chrono Trigger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chrono_Trigger). 

<figure style="width: 300px" class="align-left">
  <img src="{{ '/assets/images/lego/chrono-trigger/1.webp' | relative_url }}" >
  <figcaption>Chrono Trigger Robo</figcaption>
</figure> 

This game is so perfect that I would recommend to all kinds of people whether you are fan of which game category. In this game there is an adorable robot among the antagonists grounp. His name is Promehteus. I was so fanscinated by the game that I feel a loss when I finally made it through by a good end. Thus I want to leave a mark physically in some form. That was when I resort to LEGO IDEAS: to build part of the game by myself.  
[Robo Prometheus](https://beta.ideas.lego.com/product-ideas/204a285e-836b-4aaf-8a81-8c6b75f0d489)


The first time you make something out probably would be a rough process. In my case, I tried to build the robot as similar as I can. I made several versions to make it near. The first version was slim and seemingly delicate. It was just completely formed and looks like a robot, but too far from the character. So I tried more coloring, body shape, arm-leg ratio. Finally it's now the published one you can see.
